# PR Plan — ChaiCoin ($CHAI)

## Principles
Every PR asset is factually accurate at time of publishing. We pitch real journalists/publications with real, verifiable information. We never pay for coverage presented as independent journalism, never fabricate quotes, and never claim coverage that hasn't happened.

## Assets (see `PR/` subdirectories)
- `PR/media-kit/` — fact sheet, logo/brand assets reference, founder/project profile
- `PR/press-releases/` — drafts, clearly marked DRAFT until an actual event (e.g., testnet deployment) has occurred
- `PR/journalist-outreach/` — pitch templates and the media contact database structure
- `PR/announcements/` — launch/milestone/post-launch announcement templates
- `PR/FAQ/` — public-facing FAQ, also published on the website

## Outreach approach
1. Build a shortlist of crypto/tech journalists and publications who cover community/meme-token culture, Indian crypto policy, or small experimental projects (researched, not fabricated — see `PR/journalist-outreach/target-publications.md` for how this list should be populated with real research before any pitch is sent).
2. Personalize every pitch to the specific journalist's beat; never mass-blast identical copy.
3. Track every contact in `PR/journalist-outreach/media-database.csv` (publication, journalist, topic, contact method, date contacted, response, follow-up, status).
4. Never follow up more than twice without a response.

## What triggers a press release
- Testnet deployment (factual, technical announcement)
- Major community milestones (e.g., first successful governance poll, a security fix)
- Mainnet gate decision outcome (go or no-go), once that decision is made per `ROADMAP.md`

## What we will never do (binding)
- Pay for a placement presented as independent editorial coverage.
- Fabricate a journalist quote, publication logo use, or "featured in" claim.
- Claim government/regulatory approval or endorsement.
- Claim celebrity or influencer endorsement that hasn't been explicitly, verifiably given.
