# Marketing Plan — ChaiCoin ($CHAI)

## Principles
- Every claim must be true and verifiable at the time it's published (see `LEGAL_NOTES.md` and the ASCI compliance checklist in `research/regulations/india-vda-compliance.md` §5).
- Growth comes from real participation loops, not simulated engagement or paid hype.
- Humor and lore are the primary content engine (per `research/market/meme-token-market-2026.md`, brand/community consistency beats novelty).

## Style guide (binding)
See `marketing/strategy/style-guide.md`. Summary: warm, plain-spoken, self-aware; never "moon/100x/guaranteed"; ASCI disclaimer on every asset that mentions $CHAI; never fabricate stats.

## Phased plan

### Pre-launch (Phase 0–1, testnet stage)
Goal: build a small, real, engaged audience before there's anything to buy. Content is 100% educational/entertainment — lore, lightweight lore-comics, lore explainer, lore/lore. No "buy" call-to-action exists yet because there is nothing to buy.
- Publish brand + lore content (see `docs/BRAND.md`) across owned channels only once real accounts exist.
- Run the first meme contest with a testnet-stage framing ("help us name Kadak's tapri neighbors").
- Publish weekly build-in-public updates sourced directly from `PROJECT_STATUS.md`.

### 30-day launch window (see `marketing/content-calendar/30-day-launch-calendar.md`)
Focus: testnet deployment announcement, first Chai Break events, first quest board, transparent FAQ.

### 60-day growth plan (see `marketing/growth/60-day-growth-plan.md`)
Focus: creator/community-generated content loops, referral-free organic growth (no pay-per-signup schemes), first governance poll ("Chai Panchayat").

### 90-day roadmap (see `marketing/growth/90-day-roadmap.md`)
Focus: evaluate testnet learnings, decide (with the user's explicit approval per master brief) whether/when to pursue the mainnet gate in `ROADMAP.md`.

## Channels
X (Twitter), Instagram, YouTube Shorts/Reels, Reddit (relevant subs only, following each sub's self-promo rules), Telegram, Discord. See `social/` for platform-specific content.

## Metrics tracked (see `analytics/`)
Impressions, reach, engagement rate, follower count, community member count, website traffic, quest/contest participation — all pulled from each platform's own real analytics, never estimated or invented.

## What we will never do
Buy followers/engagement, run fake giveaways, use undisclosed AI-generated "community members," promise returns in any ad copy, omit the ASCI disclaimer, or claim media coverage/partnerships that haven't happened.
