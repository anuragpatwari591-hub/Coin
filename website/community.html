Community — The Tapri

The Tapri is ChaiCoin's community hub (Telegram/Discord). It opens once a real, available moderator can run it properly — links will appear here when that's true.

How to participate

  • Join, read the rules, and you're a Member — no token needed
  • Complete quests and enter meme contests to earn Kadak Points
