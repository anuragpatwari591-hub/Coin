The Lore of Kadak

Kadak was the last cup poured at a tapri (roadside tea stall) that was about to close for the night. Instead of going cold, the regulars — a rotating cast of strangers who'd never have met otherwise — kept the conversation going long enough that someone joked, "this cup should get a vote in how we run this place."

ChaiCoin is that joke, taken just seriously enough to build: no owner of the tapri, just whoever shows up and keeps the kettle on.
