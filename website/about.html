About ChaiCoin

ChaiCoin ($CHAI) started as a documented, research-driven project: we compared blockchains on real cost and tooling data, generated and scored ten original concepts, and picked the one that best fit our own criteria — not the flashiest idea, the most defensible one. The full process is public in our documentation.

Our first principle

We built this as if it would be reviewed by developers, journalists, security researchers, and regulators — because it might be. That means everything here is meant to be transparent, reproducible, and honest about what it is and isn't.

What we will never do

  • Promise or predict a price increase
  • Fabricate partnerships, investors, users, trading volume, or media coverage
  • Claim government or regulatory approval we haven't received
  • Hide contract mechanisms that could hurt holders

See DECISIONS.md for the full, dated decision log.
