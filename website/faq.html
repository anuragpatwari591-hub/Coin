FAQ

Is ChaiCoin live / can I buy it?

Not yet — testnet stage. Testnet tokens have no real value. Anyone offering to sell you "ChaiCoin" now is running a scam.

Will the price go up?

We don't know, and we will never claim to know. No price predictions, ever.

Can the team mint more tokens?

No mint function exists after the initial fixed supply. Verifiable in the published, verified source.

Can the team freeze my wallet?

No. No pause function, no blacklist function.

Who controls the treasury?

A multisig, not a single key. Signers published once created.

Is this audited?

Not yet by a professional third party — no budget for that at this stage, disclosed honestly. The full test suite is public.

Do I owe tax on $CHAI (once live)?

Indian taxpayers: current rules impose 30% flat tax on VDA gains and 1% TDS above thresholds, regardless of this project. Not tax advice — see our research and consult a professional.

How do I participate without buying anything?

Join the community, do quests, enter contests, vote in polls — see Community.
