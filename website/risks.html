Risks

Crypto products and NFTs are unregulated and can be highly risky. There may be no regulatory recourse for any loss from such transactions.

Project-specific risks

  • Total loss of value is possible. Most new tokens fail; ours could too. Never treat this as anything other than experimental.
  • No professional audit yet. Our test suite is public, but that is not a substitute for third-party review.
  • Early-stage / small team. Development pace, community moderation, and support may be limited or inconsistent.
  • Regulatory change. Indian (and other) VDA rules are evolving; see our compliance research.
  • Liquidity risk. Even after any future mainnet launch, there is no guarantee of deep or continuous market liquidity.

What we do to reduce (not eliminate) risk

  • Fixed supply, no mint function, no pause/blacklist — see Contract
  • Public, tested source code
  • Multisig treasury with public disbursement logging
  • Vesting on team allocation — nothing liquid at launch

None of the above eliminates risk. Never participate with money you cannot afford to lose.
