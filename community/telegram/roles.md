# Telegram Roles & Progression

| Role | How you get it | Permissions |
|---|---|---|
| Newcomer | Just joined | Read-only in main chat until rules are acknowledged |
| Member | Acknowledged rules | Full chat access, can join polls/quests |
| Chai-wale (Contributor) | Earned Kadak Points via quests/contests/helpfulness (threshold published in `community/rewards/kadak-points.md`) | Custom title tag, early access to new quest boards, voting weight in informal "Chai Panchayat" polls |
| Creator | A piece of original content adopted into official channels (meme, thread, art, video) | Pinned creator credit, invited to a private creators' chat for early briefs on contests/themes |
| Moderator | Invited by existing moderators/team after sustained, trusted contribution — never self-appointed, never a bot | Mute/ban/pin permissions, access to mod-log channel |

Role changes are manual (a human moderator updates them) until/unless a verified bot is introduced — if that happens, it will be disclosed as a bot in this document and in the bot's own Telegram profile.
