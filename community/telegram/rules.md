# The Tapri — Telegram Rules

Welcome to The Tapri, ChaiCoin's community hub. Grab a virtual cup and read this before you post.

1. **Be a decent tapri regular.** No harassment, hate speech, or targeted abuse. This is a chai stall, not a shouting match.
2. **No financial advice framed as certainty.** You can discuss the project; you cannot tell people it's guaranteed to go up, tell them to "ape in," or pressure anyone into buying anything.
3. **No scams, phishing links, or impersonation.** Nobody from the team will ever DM you first asking for your seed phrase, private key, or "wallet verification." Report anyone who does.
4. **No fabricated news.** Don't post fake partnership/listing/endorsement claims about ChaiCoin — ours or anyone else's.
5. **Mark parody as parody.** If you post a joke "breaking news" item, label it PARODY/FICTIONAL clearly.
6. **English or Hindi/Hinglish welcome.** Other languages fine if you can also summarize in one of the above so mods can moderate fairly.
7. **No spam/self-promo** of unrelated projects, referral links, or airdrop farming schemes.
8. **Mods act transparently.** Every removal/mute/ban is logged with a reason; you can appeal by DMing a moderator.

Breaking these rules gets a warning, then a temporary mute, then a ban for repeat or severe violations (scams/phishing are an instant ban, no warning).
