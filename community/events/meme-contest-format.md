# Meme Contest Format

1. **Theme announced** in #meme-contests / Telegram, with the submission window (typically 5–7 days).
2. **Submission rules:** original work or clearly-credited remixes; AI-assisted entries allowed but must be disclosed; no plagiarized art.
3. **Judging:** 70% community vote (public poll), 30% moderator sanity-check (rules compliance, not taste) to prevent brigading by throwaway accounts.
4. **Prize:** Kadak Points to all valid entries (participation), bonus Kadak Points + occasional CHAI prize (from the Community & Ecosystem allocation, per `TOKENOMICS.md` §2) to the top 1–3, announced publicly with the specific amount and tranche reference.
5. **Winning entries** get adopted into official channels/social accounts with creator credit — see `community/rewards/kadak-points.md` Creator-role path.
