# Kadak Points — Reputation System Spec

Kadak Points are non-transferable, non-sellable reputation points. They are not a token, not on-chain (at this stage), and not a security — pure reputation, per `TOKENOMICS.md` §5.

## Earning
| Action | Points |
|---|---|
| Complete onboarding | 5 |
| Helpful answer in #help (mod-confirmed) | 5–15 |
| Approved wiki/FAQ contribution | 20 |
| Contest entry (participation) | 10 |
| Contest win (per contest, judged) | 50–150 |
| Quest completion (varies by quest, published on quest board) | 10–100 |
| Sustained moderation work (monthly review) | 50–200 |

## Thresholds
- 25 points → Chai-wale (Contributor) role
- 150 points + at least one adopted original content piece → eligible for Creator role
- Sustained trusted contribution (team discretion, documented) → eligible for Moderator invite

## Transparency
- Point totals and reasons for large awards (contest wins, quest completions) are posted publicly in #quest-board / the Telegram equivalent.
- No points are ever awarded for purchasing CHAI — earning is participation-only, never pay-to-win.
- If Kadak Points are ever migrated to an on-chain (e.g., soulbound attestation) system, this document will be updated first, with a clear migration/legacy-balance policy.
