# ChaiCoin — FAQ

**Is ChaiCoin live / can I buy it?**
Not yet. As of this writing the project is at the testnet stage — see `ROADMAP.md`. Testnet tokens have no real value and are not for sale. Anyone offering to sell you "ChaiCoin" right now is running a scam.

**Will the price go up?**
We don't know, and we will never claim to know. We don't make price predictions or promise returns, ever — see `LEGAL_NOTES.md`.

**Can the team mint more tokens later?**
No. The contract has no mint function after the initial fixed supply is created at deployment. This is verifiable directly in the published, verified contract source.

**Can the team freeze my wallet or block my transfers?**
No. The contract has no pause function and no blacklist function. See `SECURITY.md` and `TOKENOMICS.md` §4.

**Who controls the treasury?**
A multisig (not a single private key), with signers publicly disclosed once created — see `TOKENOMICS.md` §3.

**Is this audited?**
Not yet by a professional third party (no budget for that at this stage — disclosed honestly, not hidden). The full test suite is public in `blockchain/tests/` so anyone can verify the claims independently.

**Is this a security / investment product?**
We do not present it as one and we do not promise returns. Whether any token is legally a "security" in a given jurisdiction is a legal question we do not resolve for you — see `LEGAL_NOTES.md` §5–6.

**Do I owe tax if I transact in $CHAI (once live)?**
If you're an Indian taxpayer, current rules impose a 30% flat tax on VDA gains and 1% TDS on transfers above threshold amounts — this applies to you personally regardless of what the project does. See `research/regulations/india-vda-compliance.md` §1. This is not tax advice; consult a professional.

**How do I participate without buying anything?**
Join the community hub once it's open, complete quests, enter meme contests, join Chai Panchayat polls — none of this requires holding $CHAI. See `COMMUNITY_PLAN.md`.

**Standard disclaimer:** Crypto products and NFTs are unregulated and can be highly risky. There may be no regulatory recourse for any loss from such transactions.
