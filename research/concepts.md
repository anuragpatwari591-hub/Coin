## Weaknesses / risks noted per concept (required by master prompt)

1. **ChaiCoin** — risk: could be seen as "just another food-meme coin" unless the utility (community reputation + governance layered on the chai-break/adda ritual) is executed with real substance, not just skin-deep branding.
2. **Jugaad** — risk: "jugaad" can also carry a negative connotation (cutting corners/rule-bending) in some professional contexts; needs careful, positive framing.
3. **VadaPav** — risk: strongly regional (Mumbai-specific) identity may limit broader relatability.
4. **Thela** — risk: weaker mascot expressiveness; harder to generate diverse meme content from a cart.
5. **Adda** — risk: an abstract noun is hard to turn into a visual mascot and merchandise/sticker content.
6. **Momo** — risk: name collision with existing recognizable "Momo" characters elsewhere reduces trademark/brand safety and search discoverability.
7. **Filter Coffee** — risk: regional (South Indian) framing narrower than a pan-Indian/global "chai" concept; ticker $FLTR reads more like a filter/tool than a token.
8. **Auto** — risk: "AUTO" as a ticker is generic and could be confused with unrelated "auto-" prefixed products (auto-trading bots etc.).
9 **Masala** — risk: word is already heavily overused across unrelated Indian-branded products; low trademark/search differentiation.
10. **Tapri** — risk: less internationally recognizable term than "chai"; smaller organic search volume.

## Selection

See `research/concept-selection.md` for the documented decision. **ChaiCoin ($CHAI)** is selected.
