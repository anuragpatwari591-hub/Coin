# Community Plan — ChaiCoin ($CHAI)

## Philosophy
"The Tapri" (our community hub) is the actual product before the token is. Per `research/market/meme-token-market-2026.md`, projects that survive are the ones with real, coordinated communities — not the ones with the cleverest contract. Everything here is designed for genuine participation; nothing here simulates engagement.

## Platforms
- **Telegram** — primary hub for real-time chat, "Chai Break" AMAs, announcements. See `community/telegram/`.
- **Discord** — structured hub for quests, roles, contribution tracking, longer-form discussion. See `community/discord/`.
- Both are opened only when a real human is ready to moderate them — an empty, unmoderated server does more brand damage than no server.

## Roles (see `community/telegram/roles.md` and `community/discord/roles.md` for platform specifics)
1. **Newcomer** — just joined, read-only guidance in onboarding channel/topic until they've read the rules.
2. **Member** — completed onboarding, can post/chat freely.
3. **Chai-wale (Contributor)** — has earned Kadak Points via real participation (quests, contests, helpful answers).
4. **Creator** — makes original content (memes, art, threads) that gets adopted into official channels.
5. **Moderator** — trusted community member (never a bot pretending to be a person) with enforcement permissions.

## Onboarding flow
1. Join → automated welcome message (clearly a bot, labeled as such) with a link to rules and a one-question "what brought you here" prompt (real signal, not a gate).
2. Read rules → react/confirm → role auto-updates to Member.
3. First-week nudge: an invitation to the next scheduled "Chai Break" and to the current quest board.

## Moderation policy (see `community/moderation/guidelines.md`)
- No tolerance for: financial advice framed as guaranteed returns, price-prediction spam, impersonation of team members, harassment, scams/phishing links.
- Moderators log every removal/ban with a reason in a private mod-log channel — never silent, unexplained removals.
- No fake engagement: no bought members, no bot accounts posing as users, no artificially inflated online-member counts.

## Events & rewards (see `community/events/` and `community/rewards/`)
- **Chai Break** — recurring (initially weekly) live text/voice AMA or open discussion slot.
- **Meme contests** — themed, judged by community vote + moderator sanity-check (no self-plagiarized/AI-ripped entries without disclosure).
- **Quests** — small real tasks (translate the FAQ, write a wiki page, report a bug, help a newcomer) that earn Kadak Points, tracked transparently in a public log once volume justifies a dashboard.
- Rewards are Kadak Points (reputation) and, per documented tranche releases (`TOKENOMICS.md` §2), occasional CHAI prizes from the Community & Ecosystem allocation — always announced publicly with the reason.

## What we will never do
- Buy followers, members, or engagement.
- Use bots to simulate a crowd.
- Pay for fake reviews or testimonials.
- Claim a community size we have not verified from the platform's own member count.
