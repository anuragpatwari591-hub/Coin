# User-Generated Content (UGC) Guidelines

- We amplify real community-created content (memes, art, threads) with creator credit — never claim it as team-made.
- We ask for explicit permission before reposting to official channels.
- We never pay for UGC in a way that isn't disclosed, and never fabricate UGC ourselves while presenting it as community-made.
- Contest-submitted content follows `community/events/meme-contest-format.md` (disclosure of AI assistance, originality requirement).
