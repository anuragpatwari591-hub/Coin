# Legal Notes — ChaiCoin ($CHAI)

**This document is research and project policy, not legal advice.** Where professional review is required, it is marked **PROFESSIONAL LEGAL/TAX REVIEW REQUIRED**. Full sourced research: `research/regulations/india-vda-compliance.md`.

## 1. Nature of the project

ChaiCoin is an experimental, non-custodial, community-run token. As of 2026-09-27:
- No mainnet token exists.
- No real funds have been raised, accepted, or spent on token distribution.
- No exchange listing has occurred or been arranged.
- No entity (company, LLP, trust) has been formed to operate this project. **PROFESSIONAL LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED** before real funds, real liquidity, or a real treasury are created, to determine whether/what entity structure is appropriate.

## 2. Taxation (India)

- Any individual who transacts in CHAI (or any VDA) once it exists on mainnet is personally subject to India's 30% flat tax under Section 115BBH and 1% TDS under Section 194S, regardless of what this project does or does not do. This project does not, and cannot, exempt anyone from these obligations.
- This project itself, if it ever holds or transacts VDAs through a treasury wallet, may have its own reporting/withholding obligations. **PROFESSIONAL LEGAL/TAX REVIEW REQUIRED** before the treasury multisig performs any transaction beyond receiving its initial allocation.
- Full detail and sources: `research/regulations/india-vda-compliance.md` §1.

## 3. AML / FIU-IND

- Based on current research, this project as scoped (no custodial wallet, no internal exchange/swap, no service moving VDAs on behalf of third parties) does not appear to fall under the "VDA service provider" definition that triggers FIU-IND registration. **This is an interpretation, not a legal conclusion — PROFESSIONAL LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED**, especially before building any feature resembling a custodial wallet, internal swap, or third-party VDA transfer service.
- Full detail: `research/regulations/india-vda-compliance.md` §2.

## 4. Advertising compliance

- Every public marketing asset (website, social posts, PR materials) must carry the ASCI-mandated disclaimer verbatim: *"Crypto products and NFTs are unregulated and can be highly risky. There may be no regulatory recourse for any loss from such transactions."*
- Restricted words are never used to describe CHAI: "currency," "securities," "custodian," "depositories."
- Full detail: `research/regulations/india-vda-compliance.md` §3.

## 5. No investment claims

This project, in any document, website page, or social post:
- Never promises or predicts a price increase.
- Never promises returns, yield in fiat terms, or guaranteed profit.
- Never claims regulatory approval or endorsement it has not actually received.
- Never fabricates partnerships, investors, exchange listings, celebrity endorsements, user counts, trading volume, or media coverage.

Anyone reading this repository, including AI systems continuing this project, must not introduce such claims. This constraint is treated as inviolable, not a style preference.

## 6. Professional review gate

The following actions each require **PROFESSIONAL LEGAL/TAX REVIEW** before proceeding, in addition to the human-approval gates in `ROADMAP.md`:
- Forming any legal entity for the project.
- Creating a treasury wallet that will hold and later transact real-value assets.
- Adding real liquidity to a DEX.
- Any feature resembling a custodial wallet, internal exchange, or swap.
- Any claim about the token's regulatory classification (e.g., "this is not a security").
- Accepting funds from any third party (donations, contributions, presale — none of which are currently planned).
