# 60-Day Growth Plan

Builds on the 30-day launch calendar. Focus shifts from "introduce the brand" to "build repeatable community loops."

## Growth loops (all organic, no paid acquisition, no bought engagement)
1. **Creator loop:** Meme/content contests every ~2 weeks → winning content gets amplified on official channels with creator credit → creators are invited into a private "creators' chat" for early contest-theme input → repeat.
2. **Quest loop:** Rotating small real tasks (see `community/rewards/kadak-points.md`) that improve the project itself (docs, translations, bug reports, FAQ answers) → Kadak Points → role progression → more investment in the community → more quest participation.
3. **Governance loop:** Regular low-stakes "Chai Panchayat" polls → visible influence on real decisions (contest themes, feature prioritization) → participants feel ownership → return for the next poll.
4. **Educational loop:** Short explainer content (tokenomics, security design, how testnet works) → builds trust with skeptical/technical audiences (journalists, developers) explicitly named as a target reviewer group in the master brief → those users are more likely to become long-term contributors, not just speculators.

## Days 31–60 milestones
- Second and third meme contests run.
- First community-nominated quest (a community member proposes a task, not just the team).
- First public "what didn't work" retrospective (e.g., if the first Chai Break had low turnout, say so and adjust the format/time).
- Evaluate whether Discord or Telegram is getting more genuine engagement and consider consolidating effort rather than spreading thin across both.
- Begin PR outreach per `PR_PLAN.md` (only to real journalists/publications, only with true claims).

## Explicitly not part of this plan
Referral-for-tokens schemes, paid influencer posts without disclosure, cross-project token-swap "partnerships" for mutual pumping, or any mechanic that creates buy pressure rather than genuine participation.
