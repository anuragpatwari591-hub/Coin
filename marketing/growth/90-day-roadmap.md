# 90-Day Roadmap (Marketing/Community Lens)

## Days 61–90
- Compile a full testnet-stage retrospective: community size (real, platform-reported), quest/contest participation, content reach, what failed and why (see `PROJECT_STATUS.md` risk log throughout).
- Decide, with the project owner's explicit sign-off, whether the project has enough genuine community traction and technical readiness to begin working through the Mainnet Gate checklist in `ROADMAP.md` Phase 2 — this is a go/no-go decision, not an assumption that 90 days automatically means "ready."
- If "go": begin the legal/economic/technical review items in `ROADMAP.md` Phase 2 in parallel with continued community growth loops.
- If "no-go" or "not yet": document why in `PROJECT_STATUS.md` and continue Phase 1 activities; there is no requirement to rush to mainnet on a fixed timeline, and doing so under pressure is explicitly discouraged by `SECURITY.md` and `LEGAL_NOTES.md`.

## Success framing (per master brief §30 — no price/profit predictions)
- **Failure scenario:** community hub sees minimal sustained activity, quests go uncompleted, no organic content emerges. Response: document honestly, do not fabricate activity to compensate, consider whether the concept/format needs to change before spending more effort.
- **Moderate adoption:** a small, real, recurring group (tens to low hundreds) actively participates in Chai Breaks/quests/contests; enough signal to justify continuing toward the Mainnet Gate review.
- **Exceptional adoption:** sustained, large, organic community growth with strong creator-generated content volume; even here, no token price, market cap, or personal profit outcome is predicted — those are separate from community-health metrics and are never promised.
