# ChaiCoin Style Guide

## Voice
Warm, plain-spoken, a little chatty, self-aware about being a meme project, radically honest. Think "your favorite tea-stall regular," not "hype-man."

## Always
- Include the ASCI disclaimer on any asset that mentions $CHAI in a promotional context: *"Crypto products and NFTs are unregulated and can be highly risky. There may be no regulatory recourse for any loss from such transactions."*
- Label parody/fictional content explicitly: **PARODY / FICTIONAL / NOT REAL NEWS**.
- Cite real numbers only, from real sources we control (our own analytics, on-chain data) or clearly attribute external sources.
- Say "we don't know yet" or "this didn't work" when true.

## Never
- Words: "moon," "100x," "guaranteed," "risk-free," "get rich," "financial freedom" (implying guaranteed outcome), "currency," "securities," "custodian," "depositories."
- Price predictions or targets, in any form (including memes/jokes that could be read as sincere).
- Claims of partnerships, investors, exchange listings, celebrity endorsements, user counts, trading volume, or media coverage that have not actually happened.
- Countdown-to-price-pump framing, fake urgency ("last chance," "price about to explode").
- Impersonation of real people, companies, exchanges, journalists, or news organizations.

## Visual
Palette and mascot per `docs/BRAND.md`. Every asset that includes the mascot uses the simple line-art style so community members can remix it.
