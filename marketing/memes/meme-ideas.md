## Guardrails for all meme content
- No price charts, no "to the moon" rockets, no implied price predictions even as a joke.
- Any joke about gains/losses stays about community effort/participation, never about token price.
- AI-assisted art is fine but disclosed when entered into official/contest channels per `community/events/meme-contest-format.md`.
