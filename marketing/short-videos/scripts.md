# Short-Video / Reels Scripts (30–45 sec each)

## 1. "Meet Kadak" (brand intro)
- **Visual:** simple animated line-art Kadak mascot, steam curling.
- **VO/caption:** "This is Kadak. Last cup poured at a tapri that almost closed. The regulars kept talking anyway. That's the whole story. That's ChaiCoin."
- **End card:** name, ticker, "brewed by the community, for the community," ASCI disclaimer in small text.

## 2. "Why No Mint Function?" (trust/education)
- **Visual:** side-by-side code snippet highlight (from `blockchain/contracts/ChaiCoin.sol`) — no `mint()` function, annotated.
- **VO/caption:** "A lot of tokens can secretly print more of themselves. Ours can't — there's literally no function for it. Don't trust us, check the code."
- **End card:** link to verified contract source.

## 3. "What Testnet Actually Means" (expectation-setting)
- **Visual:** Kadak holding a cup labeled "PRACTICE."
- **VO/caption:** "Testnet = practice money, zero real value, for testing only. If anyone tells you to buy 'testnet CHAI,' that's a scam. There is nothing to buy yet."
- **End card:** link to `ROADMAP.md` phase explainer on the website.

## 4. "A Week At The Tapri" (community recap format, recurring)
- **Visual:** quick cuts of real community screenshots (with usernames blurred/consented), meme-contest entries, quest completions.
- **VO/caption:** "Here's what actually happened this week — the good and the 'still figuring it out.'"
- **End card:** invite to next Chai Break.

All scripts follow `marketing/strategy/style-guide.md` — no price talk, no urgency framing, ASCI disclaimer on any version that mentions the token as something ownable.
