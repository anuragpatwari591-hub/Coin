# Campaign: Testnet Launch ("The Kettle's On")

**Objective:** build genuine early awareness and a real starter community before mainnet, without implying there is anything to buy or invest in.

**Duration:** Day -7 to Day +30 of the 30-day launch calendar (`marketing/content-calendar/30-day-launch-calendar.md`).

**Core message:** "We're building this in the open, on a test network, with nothing for sale — come help us test it, break it, and shape it."

**Assets used:** brand reveal thread (`social/X/launch-thread.md`), "Meet Kadak" short video (`marketing/short-videos/scripts.md` #1), meme set (`marketing/memes/meme-ideas.md`), FAQ (`PR/FAQ/faq.md`).

**Success measures (real, platform-reported only):** community hub joins, Chai Break attendance, quest/contest participation, verified testnet transactions performed by real community members. No follower-count or "reach" vanity metric is treated as success on its own without a corresponding participation metric.

**Explicitly not part of this campaign:** any giveaway requiring purchase, any paid promotion, any influencer post without clear #ad-style disclosure if ever used.
