# ChaiCoin ($CHAI) — Brand Identity

## Name & ticker
- **Project name:** ChaiCoin
- **Ticker:** $CHAI
- **Tagline:** "Brewed by the community, for the community."
- **One-line pitch:** An experimental, transparent, community-run token built around the universal ritual of the chai break — reputation, quests, and governance polls instead of price promises.

## Mascot
- **Name:** Kadak (Hindi/Hinglang for "strong" — as in "kadak chai," strong tea)
- **Form:** A terracotta kulhad (clay cup) character with a curl of steam shaped like a friendly mustache, and simple dot eyes. Deliberately simple line-art so community members with no design skill can redraw it.
- **Personality:** Warm, unpretentious, a little chatty, endlessly patient (chai always waits for you), radically honest — Kadak never promises the tea will make you rich, only that it's good company.

## Visual identity
- **Color palette** (drawn directly from chai itself, so it's explainable and not arbitrary):
  - Terracotta / Kulhad Clay — `#B5651D` (primary)
  - Milk-Chai Cream — `#F3E3C3` (background/light)
  - Masala Spice Orange — `#E07A2C` (accent/CTA)
  - Steam Grey — `#6E6E6E` (secondary text)
  - Charcoal Brew — `#2B1D14` (dark text/dark mode background)
- **Typography:** A rounded, friendly sans-serif for UI (e.g., system-ui / Inter) paired with a warm serif or hand-drawn-style display face for headings and lore text, to feel "handwritten menu board" rather than corporate.
- **Iconography:** Steam curls, kulhad cups, tea-stall bench, gossip speech bubbles.

## Tone of voice
- Warm, plain-spoken, self-aware about being a meme project. Uses humor but never lies. Never uses financial-hype language ("moon," "guaranteed," "100x") — house style explicitly bans these terms in `marketing/strategy/style-guide.md`.
- Comfortable saying "we don't know yet" or "this failed, here's what we learned."

## Social identity
- Handle pattern: `@ChaiCoinHQ` (placeholder — real handles registered only when the team is ready to operate them; no accounts are created or claimed automatically by this repository).
- Community members are called **"Chai-wale"** (tea-makers/sellers — i.e., contributors) once they've completed onboarding, and **"Newcomers"** before that (see `community/telegram/roles.md`).

## Community terminology (lore glossary)
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **The Tapri** | The community hub itself (Telegram/Discord) — named after the roadside tea stall |
| **Chai Break** | A recurring scheduled community event/AMA/discussion slot |
| **Kadak Points** | Non-monetary reputation points earned for participation (see `TOKENOMICS.md` §Utility) |
| **Chai Panchayat** | The community polling/governance mechanism (a "panchayat" is a local community council) |
| **Brewing** | In-progress features/proposals (roadmap status label) |
| **Steam Rising** | Newly launched or trending community content |

## Lore (short form; full version lives on the website Lore page)
Kadak was the last cup poured at a tapri that was about to close for the night. Instead of going cold, the regulars — a rotating cast of strangers who'd never have met otherwise — kept the conversation going long enough that someone joked "this cup should get a vote in how we run this place." ChaiCoin is that joke, taken just seriously enough to build: no owner of the tapri, just whoever shows up and keeps the kettle on.

## Brand safety notes
- No claim of returns, no price predictions, no use of ASCI-restricted words ("currency," "securities," "custodian," "depositories") anywhere in brand copy — enforced via `research/regulations/india-vda-compliance.md` §5 checklist.
- Mascot and name checked against `research/concepts.md` for collision with existing recognizable meme-coin brands; no match found as of 2026-09-27.
